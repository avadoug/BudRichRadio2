The Program Guide

Built like a cannabis newspaper for your ears.

Recurring sections give the show rhythm, make clips easier to package, and help listeners know what table they are sitting at.

01

Grow Room Truth

Cultivation lessons, real mistakes, symptoms, timing, environment, inputs, and the decisions that shape a run before the jars ever exist.

02

Hash Bench

Washing, pressing, resin quality, post-harvest handling, and the uncomfortable truth that processing exposes what the grow actually produced.

03

No Mids, No Myths

Bro-science autopsies. The place where recycled cannabis folklore gets opened up and checked for roots.

04

Breeder’s Corner

Genetics, selections, line work, keeper traits, seed projects, and the difference between making a cross and making a plan.

05

Bud Rich Philosophy

Producer thinking, abundance, patience, trust, self-sufficiency, and the long game of learning the plant instead of chasing the market.

06

Guest Cut

Interviews with growers, breeders, hash makers, educators, and culture people who have enough dirt under their fingernails to be worth hearing.

Podcast tone and vibe

Confident, grounded, and real. Educational but not academic. Cultural without being gimmicky. Grow-room conversations, not studio fluff. If it sounds like something you would hear while trimming, pressing, or checking plants, it fits.

Anchor lines
