Production Notes

Clean motion, old paper nerve, broadcast control.

The brand should feel like it knows exactly what it is doing, even when it is quiet.

Visual and motion direction

Clean, confident motion. Transitions that feel deliberate, not chaotic. Cannabis culture without stoner clichés. Modern, grounded, slightly gritty. Favor restraint over spectacle. Let silence work, especially early. Think confidence, not volume.

Music direction

Minimal hip-hop instrumental, lo-fi boom bap, or subdued electronic. Mood: thoughtful, grounded, slightly gritty. Target around 70 to 85 BPM. No melody upfront; the beat should breathe.

Do not use

Smoke wipes, neon leaf spam, cartoon stoner clichés, chaotic cuts, fake dispensary gloss, and anything louder than the host.

60-second video ad timing

0:00–0:05Open with silence and authority. Text: MOST PEOPLE BUY WEED.
0:05–0:10Beat fades in. Cut to hands in soil, trimming, tent zipper. Text: SOME PEOPLE GROW WEED.
0:10–0:15Healthy plants, trichomes, light hitting canopy. Text: THE PLANT GIVES BACK.
0:15–0:18Drop-out moment. Logo animates clean. That is what it means to be Bud Rich.
0:18–0:36Define the brand: no hype, no flex, seed to plant to harvest to jar, knowledge equals savings.
0:36–1:00Stop wasting time, stop chasing trends, stop buying mids. End with the logo and growing knowledge, not just weed.

Man in the Box

The large TV or monitor sits to the host’s right, camera-left, and is clearly visible in the same frame. The host is the physical world. The screen is the remote world. The camera captures the relationship between them. When the host turns toward the screen, the audience understands the conversation flow immediately.

Desk clutter should feel lived-in, not staged. The monitor is treated as a presence, not just a graphic.
