What it is

Cannabis education without the influencer fog machine.

Bud Rich Radio is for growers, smokers, hash makers, breeders, and curious minds who want more than hype, half-truths, jar flexes, and loud opinions. Some episodes get technical. Some get philosophical. Some are straight grow-room talk. All of it is rooted in firsthand experience and respect for the plant.

The angle

Not a flex brand. A knowledge brand.

Bud Rich is the shift from consumer thinking to producer thinking. You stop chasing trends, stop buying mids, and start trusting what you understand because you learned it from the plant.

The promise

From seed to smoke.

The show follows cannabis through the full chain: germination, growing, harvest, processing, evaluating, culture, and common sense.
