About the show

Cannabis education for people tired of hype.

Bud Rich Radio was built for growers, smokers, hash makers, breeders, and curious minds who want more than half-truths and loud opinions. This is a place for real conversations about cannabis: how it grows, how it is processed, how it is evaluated, and how it fits into real life.

Some episodes get technical. Some get philosophical. Some sound exactly like the kind of talk that happens while trimming, pressing, checking plants, or arguing over whether a plant is hungry or just being dramatic.

Bud Rich is a knowledge brand, not a jar-flex brand.

Broadcast control

The moderator is the calm hand on the wheel.

The moderator does not steal the spotlight. They make sure the spotlight lands where it matters.

The moderator is

DirectorAudience proxyPacing managerConversation catalystProblem solver

The moderator is not

LecturerDominant personalityPassive observerSilent tech ghost

Golden rule

The screen should always reflect the conversation’s focus.

Live duties

Watch pacing, energy, clarity, crosstalk, unanswered questions, and who has been left out of the conversation.

Tactical interruption

Brief, respectful, purpose-driven pauses keep the audience from getting lost in the weeds.

After show

Flag strong clips, note confusing moments, identify pacing issues, and give feedback privately.
